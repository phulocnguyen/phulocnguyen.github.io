Phu Loc Nguyen

I am a Master's student at the University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, advised by Prof. Ngan Le. I am working on medical AI, multimodal learning, and computer vision. My work has appeared at MICCAI, Medical Image Analysis (MedIA), and NeurIPS.

Before that, I obtained my B.Sc. at VNU University of Engineering and Technology in 2025, working with Dr. Tran Quoc Long.
